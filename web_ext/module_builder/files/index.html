Steenrod Module Builder

loading

Build a finite dimensional module over the Steenrod algebra by adding cells and drawing the action of the algebra generators between them. The module is checked against the Adem relations as you edit, and saves in the same JSON format as ext/steenrod_modules, so the result can be resolved by ext or opened in the Adams spectral sequence viewer.

Adding cells. Click an empty row to add a cell in that degree. Use the arrows at either end of the diagram to extend the range. Click a cell to select it, and click again to deselect; several cells can be selected at once, which is what the submodule and quotient operations act on. Press Delete to remove the selected cells or F2 (or double click) to rename one.

Drawing actions. Drag from one cell to another to toggle a term in the action of the generator whose degree is the gap between them. At the prime 2 the generators are the Sq2k: Sq1, Sq2, Sq4, Sq8, … At an odd prime they are the Bockstein β in degree 1 and the Ppk. Other operations are not free parameters — Sq3 is determined by Sq1Sq2 — so they cannot be drawn, but they are derived for you: turn on show derived actions to see them, or ask for one in the evaluator.

The evaluator. Type an operation applied to an element, such as Sq3 x1, Sq2*Sq1 x0 or Sq2 x0 + Sq1 x1, to see what your generator actions imply. Both the spacing used in the actions list (Sq3 x1) and the explicit product used by the Steenrod calculator (Sq3 * x1) are accepted.

Operations. Shift, dualise, truncate, tensor, direct sum, and the submodule generated by a selection or the quotient by it. The dual uses the antipode χ of the Steenrod algebra, not a plain transpose: transposing gives a module over the opposite algebra, and χ is what identifies that with A. So the dual can have actions the original does not — on the Joker Sq4 is zero, but on its dual it is not, because χ(Sq4) = Sq4 + Sq3Sq1.

Modules over a subalgebra. A file with a profile field, such as tmf2 or ko, is a module over a sub-Hopf-algebra rather than over the whole Steenrod algebra. Such a module can be viewed, edited and saved without losing its profile, but the relation checker does not apply to it and is switched off.
